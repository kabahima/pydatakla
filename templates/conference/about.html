About PyData Kampala

Building a thriving data science community in East Africa

What is PyData?

PyData is an educational programme of NumFOCUS, a nonprofit organisation supporting the open-source scientific computing community. PyData conferences are community-led events dedicated to sharing knowledge among practitioners of data analysis, machine learning, and other data-driven applications.

PyData Kampala Chapter

PyData Kampala is the local chapter of the global PyData community, rooted in Uganda and serving the broader East African data science ecosystem. We organise regular meetups, workshops, and an annual conference to foster knowledge-sharing and collaboration.

Our Mission

We aim to make data science education accessible to everyone in Uganda and East Africa — from students and professionals to researchers and entrepreneurs — by providing high-quality, free and low-cost events.

Get Involved

We welcome volunteers, speakers, sponsors, and participants. Whether you are just starting your data journey or are a seasoned practitioner, there is a place for you in our community.
