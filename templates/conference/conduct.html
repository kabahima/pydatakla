Community

Code of Conduct

PyData Kampala is dedicated to providing a harassment-free, productive, and inclusive experience for everyone — at our events, meetups, and online spaces.

The Short Version

Be kind to others. Do not insult or put down other attendees. Behave professionally. Harassment and sexist, racist, or exclusionary jokes and language are not appropriate for PyData events.

All attendees are expected to show respect and courtesy to others throughout the conference and at all conference events, whether officially sponsored by PyData or not. Organisers will enforce this code throughout the event.

Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as organisers and community members pledge to make participation in PyData Kampala a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Reporting an Incident

If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns, please contact a member of the conference staff immediately. Conference staff can be identified by their staff badges.

You may also report incidents directly to the PyData Kampala organisers:

conduct@pydatakampala.org

For incidents at NumFOCUS-affiliated events, you may also file a report directly with NumFOCUS at numfocus.org/report. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and all reporters have the right to confidentiality.

Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all PyData Kampala community spaces — including the conference venue, workshops, social events, and online channels — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the NumFOCUS Code of Conduct and the PyData Code of Conduct. PyData Kampala is a community event organised under the NumFOCUS umbrella.
